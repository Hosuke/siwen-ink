皐門

A definition of "皐門" (city gate) from the *Book of Songs*, describing the Zhou dynasty's fortified outer gates as grand, majestic structures built for both defense and public safety.

廼立皐門，皐門有伉。廼立應門，應門將將。

皐門者，郭門也。周立郭門，外以禦暴，內以安民。伉，高大也。言其制雄壯。

所出：《詩經 · 緜》 — https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/詩經/緜
