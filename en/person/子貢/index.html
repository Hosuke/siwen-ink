Zigong

子貢

Also known as: 端木賜

Disciple of Confucius, also known as Duanmu Ci. Received texts portray him in discussions of moral conduct, diplomacy, and public affairs.

Appearances (40 entries)

  • 子貢存魯亂齊破吳強晉霸越 — 記端木賜一出而五國形勢俱變之事。
  • 子貢殖 — 子貢結駟連騎，束帛之幣以聘享諸侯，使孔子名布揚於天下。
