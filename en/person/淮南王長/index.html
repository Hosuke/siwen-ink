Liu Chang

淮南王長

c.180-127 BCE

Han prince whose conflict with the imperial court is described in received histories. The indexed account uses his fate in a discussion of political conduct.

Appearances (2 entries)

  • 淮南王長廢法 — 淮南王長廢先帝法，謀反，孝文不忍誅之，赦罪廢王，後追謚厲王。
  • 不食而死 — 淮南王長以驕致禍，絕食以死明志。
