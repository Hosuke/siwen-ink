Duke Wen of Jin

文公

c.697-628 BCE

Ruler of Jin in the Spring and Autumn period. Received histories recount his years in exile and subsequent rise to power.

Appearances (40 entries)

  • 秦文公 — 記秦文公東獵居汧渭、設鄜畤、收周餘民、立法誅三族之事。
