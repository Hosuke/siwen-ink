高以下為基

The article explains that true elevation or power must be grounded in humility, as the Laozi and its commentaries (e.g., the “He‑Shang‑Gong” commentary) teach that a solid, lasting high position can only be built on a strong, humble foundation.

「高以下為基」，言欲居上位者，以下為基。[[昔之得一]]河上公注云「猶築牆造功，因卑成高，下不堅固，後必傾危」。[[貴以賤為本]]
