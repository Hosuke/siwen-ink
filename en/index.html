Classics

  • Analects 論語 — A collection of sayings and conversations associated with Confucius and his disciples, concerning learning, ritual, humane conduct, and government.
  • Great Learning 大學 — A Confucian text linking self-cultivation with the ordering of family, state, and world.
  • Doctrine of the Mean 中庸 — A Confucian text on sincerity, harmony, and moral cultivation, traditionally associated with Zisi.
  • Mencius 孟子 — A collection of dialogues and arguments associated with Mengzi, addressing humane government, moral development, human nature, and the responsibilities of ruler…
  • Classic of Filial Piety 孝經 — A Confucian classic presented as a dialogue on filial piety, linking family duties with ethical conduct, education, and political order.
  • Erya 爾雅 — An early Chinese glossary that groups and explains words for kinship, buildings, landscapes, plants, animals, and other categories, drawing on classical usage.
  • Book of Rites 禮記 — A Confucian collection of writings on ritual, mourning, music, education, and social relationships, transmitted as one of the major ritual classics.
  • Rites of Zhou 周禮 — A Confucian ritual classic describing an ideal administrative order, official responsibilities, ritual institutions, and technical offices.
  • Book of Etiquette and Ceremonial 儀禮 — One of the Confucian ritual classics, describing ceremonies for capping, marriage, mourning, sacrifice, archery, and formal hospitality.
  • Book of Changes (Yijing) 周易 — A classical collection of hexagrams, line statements, and interpretive writings.
  • Book of Odes 詩經 — An early Chinese anthology of songs, court poems, and ritual hymns.
  • Book of Documents 尚書 — A Confucian classic of speeches, proclamations, and accounts of early rulers.
  • Zuo Commentary on the Spring and Autumn Annals 春秋左氏傳 — A historical narrative associated with the Spring and Autumn Annals, describing interstate relations, political events, speeches, and ritual judgments.
  • Gongyang Commentary on the Spring and Autumn Annals 春秋公羊傳 — A commentary on the Spring and Autumn Annals that draws political and ethical meanings from the chronicle's wording through a question-and-answer format.
  • Guliang Commentary on the Spring and Autumn Annals 春秋穀梁傳 — A commentary on the Spring and Autumn Annals interpreting its terse entries through ritual norms, judgments of conduct, and principles of political order.
  • Tao Te Ching (Wang Bi Edition) 道德經 (王弼本) — The received Laozi text with Wang Bi's third-century commentary, discussing the Dao, non-action, government, and the relation between being and nonbeing.
