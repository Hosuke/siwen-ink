The Laozi of the Silk Manuscripts 帛書老子杳眇注

杳眇 · with Yaomiao's Reading · 44 chapters of De, 37 of Dao · 81 chapters

The Mawangdui silk manuscripts, buried in 168 BCE, place the Book of De before the Book of Dao. This edition follows them, with the Wang Bi recension as collation. Each of the eighty-one chapters gives the silk text with an English rendering, character niches that open the key graphs through their seal forms and the Shuowen, and Yaomiao's reading in classical Chinese with its English translation.
